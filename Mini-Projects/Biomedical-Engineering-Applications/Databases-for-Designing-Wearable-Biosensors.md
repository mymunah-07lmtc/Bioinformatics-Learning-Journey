# Biomedical Engineering Applications

## Databases for Designing Wearable Biosensors
- **BME problem**: Continuous monitoring of biomarkers (e.g., glucose, lactate, cortisol) in sweat or interstitial fluid.
- **Bioinformatics input**:
  - **UniProt** + **PDB**: Identify proteins (enzymes, antibodies) that specifically bind the target molecule.
    - Example: Glucose oxidase (UniProt P81156) – structure (PDB: 1GAL) shows the active site where glucose binds.
  - Engineers immobilize this enzyme on an electrode – electron transfer produces a measurable current.
  - **NCBI** provides genetic sequences to recombinantly produce stable, thermotolerant enzyme variants.
- **Future direction**:
  - **Machine learning** (using sequence databases) to predict which enzyme mutations improve sensor stability.
  - Integration with **wireless transmission** – part of the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT).
