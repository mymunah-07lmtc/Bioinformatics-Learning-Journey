# Disease of the Month

## Month 5: Alzheimer’s Disease
- **Molecular basis**: Accumulation of amyloid‑β (Aβ) plaques (from APP cleavage) and hyperphosphorylated tau tangles. Genetic risk: *APOE* ε4 allele, *APP*, *PSEN1*, *PSEN2* mutations.
- **Bioinformatics tools used**:
  - **NCBI Gene**: APP (ID: 351) – see transcripts, exons. PSEN1 (ID: 5663) – presenilin, part of γ‑secretase complex.
  - **UniProt**: Amyloid‑β precursor protein (P05067) – view cleavage sites for β‑ and γ‑secretase. Tau (P10636) – microtubule‑associated protein.
  - **PDB**: Aβ fibril structures (e.g., 2BEG) – used to design aggregation inhibitors.
  - **BLAST**: Compare APP across species – conserved domains hint at physiological functions beyond Alzheimer’s.
- **BME connections**:
  - **Biomarkers**: Detection of Aβ and tau in cerebrospinal fluid or blood using antibody‑based sensors.
  - **Drug delivery**: Nanoparticles crossing the blood‑brain barrier to deliver anti‑aggregation agents.
  - **Neuroprosthetics**: Understanding cholinergic pathways lost in Alzheimer’s – electrical stimulation for memory enhancement (experimental).
