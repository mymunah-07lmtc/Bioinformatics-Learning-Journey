# Disease of the Month

## Month 4: Type 2 Diabetes
- **Molecular basis**: Multifactorial – insulin resistance in peripheral tissues and relative insulin deficiency. Key genes: *TCF7L2*, *PPARG*, *KCNJ11*, and *INS* (rare forms).
- **Bioinformatics approach**:
  - **NCBI dbGaP**: Genome‑wide association studies (GWAS) for T2D risk loci.
  - **UniProt**: Explore insulin (P01308), insulin receptor (P06213), and GLUT4 (P14672).
  - **BLAST**: Compare insulin sequences across species (all very similar – reflects conserved function).
  - **PDB**: Insulin receptor bound to insulin (PDB: 3LOH) – structural basis for signal transduction.
- **BME applications**:
  - **Continuous glucose monitors (CGMs)** – sensor technology and data algorithms.
  - **Insulin pumps** – closed‑loop artificial pancreas systems.
  - **Islet encapsulation** – bioengineered materials to protect transplanted insulin‑producing cells.
  - **Predictive models** – machine learning to forecast glucose levels.
