# Protein of the Week

## Week 7: Myoglobin
- **Function**: Oxygen storage in muscle tissues – releases O₂ during intense activity.
- **Structure**: Single globin chain with a heme group (similar to one subunit of hemoglobin).  
  *PDB ID: 1MBO* (sperm whale myoglobin – classic structure).
- **Bioinformatics comparison**:
  - **UniProt** (P02144 for human): Compare with hemoglobin α/β chains using BLAST – they share the globin fold but have different regulatory properties.
  - **PDB**: View the distal histidine (His64) that stabilizes bound oxygen – a key residue for O₂ affinity.
  - **NCBI**: Explore mutations that cause myoglobinuria (muscle breakdown).
- **BME link**:
  - **Oxygen carriers**: Engineering myoglobin variants as blood substitutes or for tissue engineering – to improve O₂ delivery to hypoxic regions.
  - **Biosensors**: Myoglobin is a marker for acute myocardial infarction; designing electrochemical sensors using its redox properties.
  - **Biomaterials**: Myoglobin incorporated into hydrogels to mimic muscle oxygenation in vitro.
