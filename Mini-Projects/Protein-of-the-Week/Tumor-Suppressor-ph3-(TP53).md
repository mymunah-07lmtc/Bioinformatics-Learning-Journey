# Protein of the Week

## Week 5: Tumor Suppressor p53 (TP53)
- **Function**: “Guardian of the genome” – regulates cell cycle arrest, DNA repair, and apoptosis in response to cellular stress.
- **Structure**: Tetrameric transcription factor with a DNA‑binding domain, a transactivation domain, and a C‑terminal regulatory region.  
  *PDB ID: 1TUP* (DNA‑binding domain bound to DNA).
- **Bioinformatics exploration**:
  - **UniProt** (P04637): View the full sequence, post‑translational modifications (phosphorylation, acetylation), and cancer‑associated mutants.
  - **NCBI Gene**: TP53 (ID: 7157) – see genomic location (17p13.1), exons, and isoforms.
  - **NCBI ClinVar**: Search “TP53” – thousands of pathogenic variants linked to Li‑Fraumeni syndrome and sporadic cancers.
  - **BLAST**: Compare p53 across vertebrates – the DNA‑binding domain is highly conserved.
- **BME relevance**: 
  - **Gene therapy**: Delivering wild‑type p53 via viral vectors (e.g., adenovirus) to restore tumor suppression.
  - **Small molecules**: Drugs like nutlin‑3a that block the MDM2‑p53 interaction (structure‑guided design using PDB interfaces).
  - **Biosensors**: Detecting mutant p53 in liquid biopsies for early cancer diagnosis.
