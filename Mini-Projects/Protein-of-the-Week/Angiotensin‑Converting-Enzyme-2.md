# Protein of the Week

## Week 6: ACE2 (Angiotensin‑Converting Enzyme 2)
- **Function**: Membrane‑bound enzyme that cleaves angiotensin II (blood pressure regulation) and serves as the entry receptor for SARS‑CoV‑2.
- **Structure**: Two domains – a peptidase domain (extracellular) and a collectrin‑like domain. The virus spike protein binds the peptidase domain.  
  *PDB ID: 6M17* (ACE2 bound to the SARS‑CoV‑2 spike receptor‑binding domain).
- **Bioinformatics steps**:
  - **UniProt** (Q9BYF1): Annotates the signal peptide, active sites (zinc‑binding motif), and known variants.
  - **NCBI**: View the ACE2 gene (ID: 59272) – located on X chromosome, explains gender differences in COVID‑19 severity.
  - **BLAST**: Compare ACE2 across mammals – residues that interact with the virus are variable, explaining species susceptibility.
- **BME applications**:
  - **Soluble ACE2 decoys**: Engineered proteins that bind and neutralize the virus – designed using PDB structures.
  - **Tissue engineering**: ACE2 is expressed in lung, heart, kidney – important for organ‑on‑a‑chip models of COVID‑19.
  - **Drug delivery**: Nanoparticles decorated with ACE2 fragments to target virus‑infected cells.
