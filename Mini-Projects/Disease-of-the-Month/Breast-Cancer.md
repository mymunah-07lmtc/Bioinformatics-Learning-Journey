# Disease of the Month

## Month 3: Breast Cancer (focus on HER2‑positive subtype)
- **Molecular basis**: Amplification of the **ERBB2** gene (on chromosome 17) → overexpression of HER2 protein (a receptor tyrosine kinase).
- **Consequence**: Uncontrolled cell growth, aggressive tumor phenotype.
- **Bioinformatics exploration**:
  - **NCBI Gene**: ERBB2 (ID: 2064) – view genomic location, transcript variants.
  - **UniProt** (P04626): HER2 extracellular domain, kinase domain, and known mutations.
  - **PDB structure**: Trastuzumab (Herceptin) bound to HER2 (PDB: 1N8Z) – shows how monoclonal antibody blocks receptor dimerization.
  - **PubMed search**: “HER2 targeted therapy” – many clinical trials.
- **BME applications**:
  - **Monoclonal antibodies** (Trastuzumab, Pertuzumab) – protein engineering to improve affinity.
  - **Antibody‑drug conjugates** (Enhertu) – targeted chemotherapy.
  - **Biosensors**: Detection of HER2 overexpression in liquid biopsies.
