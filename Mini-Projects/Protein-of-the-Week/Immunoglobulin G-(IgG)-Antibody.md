# Protein of the Week

## Week 8: Immunoglobulin G (IgG) Antibody
- **Function**: Humoral immunity – neutralizes pathogens, opsonizes antigens, activates complement.
- **Structure**: Y‑shaped tetramer (2 heavy chains, 2 light chains) with variable domains (antigen binding) and constant domains (effector functions).  
  *PDB ID: 1IGT* (intact IgG1).
- **Bioinformatics resources**:
  - **UniProt**: Multiple entries – e.g., human IgG1 heavy chain (P01857) and kappa light chain (P01834). Note the constant region allotypes.
  - **IMGT** (not NCBI, but useful): View the immunoglobulin gene locus and somatic hypermutation.
  - **NCBI**: Search for “monoclonal antibody sequences” in GenBank – many therapeutic antibodies (e.g., trastuzumab, adalimumab).
  - **PDB**: Thousands of antibody‑antigen complexes – helps understand epitope recognition.
- **BME impact**:
  - **Therapeutic antibodies**: Protein engineering to improve affinity, reduce immunogenicity, or create bispecific antibodies (e.g., for cancer immunotherapy).
  - **Biosensors**: Antibody‑based ELISA and surface plasmon resonance for biomarker detection.
  - **Drug conjugates**: Antibody‑drug conjugates (ADCs) – targeted chemotherapy (e.g., Enhertu).
